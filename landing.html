What you get

Everything you need to stay on track

From quick entry to smart alerts — budgetje covers the full picture every month.

⚡
Quick Entry Form
Log any expense, income, or savings in seconds. Pick type → category → item → amount. Done.
🔄
Auto-fill Actuals
Every transaction instantly updates your budget table actuals. No double entry, ever.
📋
Full Monthly Budget
Plan income, 9 expense categories, and savings goals — all side-by-side with actuals and variance.
📊
Visual Charts
Three live charts: Projected vs Actual, Expense Distribution (doughnut), and Savings Progress.
🚨
Over-Budget Alerts
Red badge on the Budget tab counts over-budget line items. Affected rows are highlighted red.
☁️
Cloud Sync
Sign in with Google to sync all your months across every device in real time via Firestore.
✏️
Edit Transactions
Tap the pencil on any log entry to update amount, category, date, or note — even change months.
📅
Copy Last Month
One tap copies all budget targets from the previous month — no retyping every January.
🌙
Dark Mode
Light or dark — your preference is saved. Looks great on both phone and desktop.
